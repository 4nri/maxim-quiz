# Mini Maxim

Created with the built-in image_gen tool from a user-supplied portrait and approved for use in this quiz app. Original portrait is not included. PNGs retain their generated alpha channel, without image editing. This is an original mascot, not an official Roblox or Minecraft asset.

Files: `ready.png` (welcome), `happy.png` (high five), `think.png` (encouragement). Badges are code-drawn SVG overlays in `game.js`, not part of the generated PNGs.

## Base prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one delightful original full-body small mascot for this child's educational quiz web app, 'Mini Maxim', inspired by the chunky block-built avatar aesthetic of Roblox and Minecraft. Input image is a subject/likeness reference ONLY: use the boy in the foreground, ignore the entire background and other people. Preserve recognizable age-appropriate facial likeness: tousled side-swept light brown hair, brown-hazel eyes, fair skin, soft face shape and characteristic eyebrows, nose and mouth, translated into an appealing stylized 3D game avatar. Face must be sculpted/cartoon stylized, never a photograph pasted on a cube. Large subtly rounded cuboid head, chunky cuboid torso, short blocky limbs, friendly confident small smile, waving one hand to welcome the learner. Keep the distinctive violet-purple sweater with simplified black abstract accents and white collar from the reference, add simple dark trousers and white sneakers. Full body entirely visible, centered, gentle 3/4 view while looking at viewer. Polished toy-like low-poly 3D render, clean silhouette readable at 96px, simple geometry with enough facial detail for resemblance, soft studio lighting, cheerful but not babyish. Single character only. Genuine transparent background, no scene, no ground plane, no lettering, no watermark, no logos, no weapons, no other characters. Square composition with generous transparent margins.

Keep identical rendering style, studio lighting and body proportions.
